# Scenario 15: slip lanes and cut (splayed) corners, lab log (21 Nov 2026)
Thread "Make kerbs work at slip lanes and cut corners", reports to kerb lead 5. Rule 7: one hypothesis at a time, expected effect, every labelled crop.
Inputs: nsw-2026-11-20-h (3,282 lines, no per-line method), NSW road centrelines (Transport Theme layer 5) for its 320 cells.
Known fails (KN25, P-0243): Eastgardens MGA 335390,6243297; Kogarah 326496,6238117; Waterloo 151.2129,-33.9104.
## Round 1 (diagnose)
- Looked at the 3 known fails on aerial with centrelines + lots (sheet1).
- H1: slip lanes leave a small closed face in the centreline network (the traffic island between the slip lane and the two roads). A kerb line that ENTERS such a face through its edge (not a ring wholly inside it) is cutting across a slip lane. Faces 30-4000 m2: 6,715; lines entering one by >3 m: 64 (of 234 face hits). Expected: the 3 known fails among them, and most of the 64 odd.
- H1 result: FAIL. Eastgardens and Kogarah have NO island face: NSW centrelines do not draw these slip lanes as their own segments. Faces of island size (<1,500 m2) that a line enters: 15, all park footpath loops (fh 9-11 not filtered) or small blocks; 0 slip lanes. Bigger faces (<4,000 m2): small city blocks. Why: the centreline layer is not a carriageway model; slip lanes exist only where the road authority mapped them as separate roads.
- H2: a cut corner sits much farther from the lots than the same line's own verge along the street (local 30th percentile over 60 m; flag >5 m above for 8 m+). First run lost most lots (key case objectid vs OBJECTID, my bug). Fixed run: 511 stretches on 417 lines, Eastgardens/Kogarah/Waterloo NOT flagged. Profile: Eastgardens line 1266 is 8 m from lots along the west verge and 9-10 m at the corner (+2 m only), Kogarah line 2261 6.5 m -> 8.7 m at 180 m. FAIL: the cadastre flares with the junction, so lot distance cannot see a corner that dips into the carriageway.
- Diagnosis Eastgardens (aerial, e70): street-grid style line, west side in the grass verge ~3 m behind the kerb (OK by the 2D bar), corner dips 2-4 m into the flared junction asphalt. Waterloo point is 26 m from the nearest -h line (line 1161): the case spot needs its exact line from the lead.
- LiDAR intensity profile Eastgardens (igrids2 335_6243, +-8 m perpendicular every 10 m): range 2-33, no clean asphalt/verge edge on the OK stretch either (agrees with KN25).

## Round 7 (H7, rebuilt return from the two straight arms; approved by lead 5)
- Method: fit straight arms 6-16 m either side of a turn (>=40 deg, arms straight to 0.6 m), intersect them, implied fillet radius r = gap(apex to arm intersection) / (1/cos(turn/2) - 1). Expect a real kerb corner r about 2-12 m and a cut or chord larger.
- Known answer, council kerbs: CBC 94 usable corners, r median 6.6 (5-95 %: 4.0-18.1), 11 % over 14 m; Parramatta only 6 usable (lines are short pieces, 120 corners sit within 16 m of a line end). Council corners are not all good, so this is a spread, not a pass bar.
- Labels (-h, nsw-2026-11-20, KN23 dropped geometry): OK corners 189 usable of 227: r median 4.0, 5-95 % 3.9-6.3, gap 1.0-3.0. Our own corners are code-built fillets of a fixed 4 m radius, so shape carries no signal. Odd corners: s07 turn 30 deg and s33 turn 23 deg are KINKS under the 40 deg floor (no corner at all); s36 r=4.0 gap 3.0 = looks exactly like an OK corner; c5-c40 arm not straight; 3 others had no usable turn.
- RESULT: H7 FAILS (recall 1 of 7 flagged as a corner at all, and that one has OK-like numbers; the OK corners span the same r). Finding for the lead: the odd corners are not wrong in the corner shape. The built fillet is always r=4; what is wrong is where the two ARMS sit (4 m south of the real corner at s36, a lot-frontage kink at s07, a strip-parcel diagonal at s33). So any fix must look at arm position against the street's real kerb, not at the corner.
- Tried and failed so far: H1-H7 (each in the log). Untested: measure each arm against the street kerb a second way (LiDAR step per arm, done as S12 for sides; corners are too short), or build corners only where BOTH arms are already verified and drop the corner piece elsewhere (cut, keep the arms).
## Round 8 (H8, per-arm check: canopy H4 road-edge walk reused; lead 5's order)
- Method: every 2 m along the line, nearest NSW centreline (fh<=7, datum-corrected roads), walk out toward the line on LiDAR ground intensity (road = within 2.5 MAD of that km cell's centreline median), road edge = first 1 m of non-road after 1.5 m; offset = line distance - edge. Grids: igrids kn9/kn13/kn22 (intensity only; first-kerb-rise half of H5 not used, no ground grids unpacked). Rule: 20 m windows every 10 m (>=5 of 10 samples seen), window off when median |offset| > 3 m; flag a run of >=3 windows (40 m+). Code s15-code/h8.py.
- Known answer first (40 OK -h lines, 120-500 m, offset_curve moved): 0 m: 4 % of windows off, 2 % of lines flagged; 3 m toward road: 57 %; 5 m: 32 % (coverage falls to 18 % once the line is beyond the walk); 5 m away: 87 %. Per-sample runs (no window median) catch only 0-5 %: too noisy, so the window median is needed.
- Labels (coverage: 5 of 7 odd corners and 270 OK crops have grids and >=20 of 40 samples): odd corners 0 of 5 flagged (s36, x05, c29, c4-c13, c5-c40; s07/s33 have no grid). They are 10-20 m faults, a 40 m rule cannot see them by design; at 2 windows only x05 flags. OK crops flagged: 15 of 270 (5.6 %).
- S11 whole-line list: flagged 2818 (60-110 m), 2376 (350-460 m), 2950 (190-270 m) = 3 of 8; 2799, 2545, 2847, 2608 pass (median offsets -0.3 to -1.3 m, i.e. the line is within the walked road edge, so the walk sees it as good); 1925 (Penrith) has 6 of 160 samples (intensity far off the calibration).
- Thresholds (3/4/5 m x 2/3 windows): 4 m: S11 1/8, OK flagged 5/270; 3 m x 3: S11 3/8, odd corners 0/5, OK flagged 15/270 (precision about 3 of 18 if every flagged OK crop is truly OK).
- RESULT: H8 does NOT separate the odd corners and flags only 3 of 8 whole-line faults. It works as a coarse whole-line off-road screen (known-answer recall 57-87 % at 3-5 m), not for corners. Why corners fail: the arms are right to within the road-edge noise (median offsets -1.0 to -1.7 m); the fault is a short stretch (kink, strip parcel, chord), not an offset arm. Why 4 S11 lines pass: the reviewer's "3-5 m in trees / wall / bays" lines sit near the walked edge (as the canopy card found: aerial labels under trees may be wrong or the walk sticks to lawns).
