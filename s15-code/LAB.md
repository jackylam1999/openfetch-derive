# Scenario 15: slip lanes and cut (splayed) corners, lab log (21 Nov 2026)
Thread "Make kerbs work at slip lanes and cut corners", reports to kerb lead 5. Rule 7: one hypothesis at a time, expected effect, every labelled crop.
Inputs: nsw-2026-11-20-h (3,282 lines, no per-line method), NSW road centrelines (Transport Theme layer 5) for its 320 cells.
Known fails (KN25, P-0243): Eastgardens MGA 335390,6243297; Kogarah 326496,6238117; Waterloo 151.2129,-33.9104.
## Round 1 (diagnose)
- Looked at the 3 known fails on aerial with centrelines + lots (sheet1).
- H1: slip lanes leave a small closed face in the centreline network (the traffic island between the slip lane and the two roads). A kerb line that ENTERS such a face through its edge (not a ring wholly inside it) is cutting across a slip lane. Faces 30-4000 m2: 6,715; lines entering one by >3 m: 64 (of 234 face hits). Expected: the 3 known fails among them, and most of the 64 odd.
- H1 result: FAIL. Eastgardens and Kogarah have NO island face: NSW centrelines do not draw these slip lanes as their own segments. Faces of island size (<1,500 m2) that a line enters: 15, all park footpath loops (fh 9-11 not filtered) or small blocks; 0 slip lanes. Bigger faces (<4,000 m2): small city blocks. Why: the centreline layer is not a carriageway model; slip lanes exist only where the road authority mapped them as separate roads.
- H2: a cut corner sits much farther from the lots than the same line's own verge along the street (local 30th percentile over 60 m; flag >5 m above for 8 m+). First run lost most lots (key case objectid vs OBJECTID, my bug). Fixed run: 511 stretches on 417 lines, Eastgardens/Kogarah/Waterloo NOT flagged. Profile: Eastgardens line 1266 is 8 m from lots along the west verge and 9-10 m at the corner (+2 m only), Kogarah line 2261 6.5 m -> 8.7 m at 180 m. FAIL: the cadastre flares with the junction, so lot distance cannot see a corner that dips into the carriageway.
- Diagnosis Eastgardens (aerial, e70): street-grid style line, west side in the grass verge ~3 m behind the kerb (OK by the 2D bar), corner dips 2-4 m into the flared junction asphalt. Waterloo point is 26 m from the nearest -h line (line 1161): the case spot needs its exact line from the lead.
- LiDAR intensity profile Eastgardens (igrids2 335_6243, +-8 m perpendicular every 10 m): range 2-33, no clean asphalt/verge edge on the OK stretch either (agrees with KN25).
## Round 2 plan
- H3 aerial edge test at corners only: a kerb has dark asphalt on the road side and a brighter or greener surface within 1-3 m on the land side; a corner cutting into the carriageway has asphalt on both sides. Test only on curved stretches (turning > 30 deg within 25 m), where the bad cuts live, so the long straight sides that beat KN19's colour test are out of scope. Expected: Eastgardens corner flags, its straight sides do not; then run on every corner of -h and blind-pack what it flags and what it keeps.
## Round 2 (H3, aerial surface on corners; approved by lead 5)
- Test: corner stretch = heading turns >30 deg within +-10 m (plus 10 m each side); every 2 m sample both bands 0.5-3 m off the line (z19 NSW imagery); flag when both sides read asphalt (>70 % of pixels) for 6 m+.
- v1 asphalt = grey (max-min<28, 25<mean<115). Eastgardens corner flags (both sides 1.0 at 320-360 m, verge side 0.0-0.1 on its good stretch). Known answer, Parramatta council kerbs (2x2 km, 127 corners): 34 flagged (27 %): grey concrete footpaths and tree shadow read as asphalt.
- v2 (diagnosed from pixel medians: Parramatta asphalt ~(104,98,98), Eastgardens asphalt ~(41,45,49), grass verge ~(95,95,72), concrete ~(181,170,159)): asphalt must be blue-neutral (B - (R+G)/2 >= -1), mean 45-130 (darker = shadow, unknown). Parramatta 1 of 127 flagged, Eastgardens corner still flags (14 m run).
- Second known answer, Canterbury-Bankstown kerbs (1.2 km box near Bankstown): 40 of 210 corners flagged (19 %); at road junctions only (within 30 m of a 3-way centreline node): 19 of 111 (17 %). Looked at 16: car-park kerbs, tree shade on the verge, and shaded grey concrete footpath right at the kerb. FAIL: single-date RGB cannot tell asphalt from shaded concrete or shade; Parramatta's 1 % was luck of the place.
- Checked official NSW Transport Theme for a carriageway or island reference: layers are points/lines only (TrafficControlDevice, TransportFacilityLine 0 near Eastgardens). None.
## Round 3 plan
- H4 kerb step at corners from LiDAR ground height (0.5 m grids, 93 tiles from round2/topup: inner east and inner west, incl. Waterloo 334_6246 and CBC 328-329/6244-6247, NOT Eastgardens 335_6243): a kerb has a 0.08-0.2 m rise within 1.5 m of the line; a corner cut into the carriageway is smooth. Known answer first on CBC council corners inside those tiles, then -h corners there. Height is not fooled by shade or concrete, which beat H3. Limit: only where ground grids exist (ordering more LiDAR needs Jacky).
